Metric Time & Date System

A Simpler, Logical Way to Measure Time

Why Metric Time?

The Metric Time & Date System reimagines how we measure time using base-10 divisions. It replaces archaic, inconsistent units with a clear, logical framework aligned with natural cycles—the Day and the Year.

Time Structure

  • Day: 1 full rotation of the Earth
  • 10 mHours per Day
  • 100 mMinutes per mHour
  • 100 mSeconds per mMinute

For example:

  • Noon = 5.00 mHours
  • 9:00 PM = 7.5 mHours

Date Structure

  • Year: 1 orbit around the Sun
  • 10 mMonths per Year
  • Each mMonth = 36 days (or 37 in select months to match 365-day solar year)
  • Each mMonth has 6 mWeeks (6-day weeks)
  • Each mWeek = 4 work days + 2 rest days

mMonth Names

  1. Renewal
  2. Awaken
  3. Bloom
  4. Thrive
  5. Radiate
  6. Sustain
  7. Gather
  8. Reflect
  9. Diminish
  10. Restore

Key Benefits

  • Simplicity: No more 24/60/60 conversions. Decimal math is easy and intuitive.
  • Digital Friendly: Designed for clean integration with computers and programming.
  • Global Consistency: Aligns human activity with a universal system.
  • Education: Easier to teach and learn for both children and adults.
  • Work-Life Balance: 4 days on / 2 days off creates better rhythms and more recovery.

Conversion Examples

Time:

  • 1 mHour = 2.4 conventional hours
  • 1 mMinute = 1.44 conventional minutes
  • 1 mSecond = 0.864 conventional seconds

Date:

  • Traditional January ≈ Renewal (mMonth 01)
  • Traditional February ≈ Awaken (mMonth 02)
  • ... etc.
